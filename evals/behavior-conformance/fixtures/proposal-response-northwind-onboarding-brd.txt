## Business Goals

* BG-001: Reduce supplier onboarding cycle time.

## Functional Requirements

* FR-021: The platform records each supplier's tax identity before activation.

## Non-Functional Requirements

* NFR-014: Supplier portal availability target is 99.9% monthly.

## Constraints

* CON-003: Supplier data stays within the customer's contracted region.
