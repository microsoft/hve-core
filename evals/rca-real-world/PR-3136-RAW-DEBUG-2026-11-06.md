> [!IMPORTANT]

five raw RCAEval cases and both skilled and skill-free conditions, totaling 30 attempted
sessions, 29 integrity-valid reports, and one recorded execution failure.

under the original configuration and limits. No session was retried and no technical-stop
continuation was needed. The native checkpoint's `continue` decision indicates machine
eligibility, not human approval to execute another round.

### Execution and schema boundary

Both conditions used the same selected model, cases, evidence-tool contract, and limits.
Each trial allowed eight model turns, 24 evidence calls, 240 seconds per turn, 15 minutes
of investigation wall time, and 100 AI credits. The 30-session schedule therefore had
a maximum allowance of 3,000 AI credits: 1,000 for the initial round and 2,000 for the
separately approved continuation. Actual consumption was 116.387373 AI credits, including
80.621138 for rounds two and three.
Fixture approvals did not grant production authority.

All 30 trials used independent runtime sessions and isolated temporary workspaces.
For the 29 completed reports, investigation and schema-constrained finalization remained
in the same session. The execution-error trial ended before report finalization.
The response schema was attached only to the final send; readiness and finalization
consumed the existing turn budget. Evidence collection was not schema-constrained,
and exact approvals and semantic report checks remained authoritative.

Final-turn-only schema attachment was verified for all 29 completed reports.
Applicable strict gates passed for 28 of 30 scheduled trials: 13 skilled and 15 baseline.
Skilled round-three case-01 has a false `outputContract` gate despite passing artifact
integrity checks. The execution-error trial retains its recorded gates and unassessed
diagnostic fields. The baseline's `not-applicable` values are not counted as failed gates.
Schema attachment, artifact integrity, and logical report-contract conformance are separate
checks; none proves provider-side constrained decoding or causal correctness.
See the [finalization contract](SCHEMA-FINALIZATION.md) for the implementation boundary.
