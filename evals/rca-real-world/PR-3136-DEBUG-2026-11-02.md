## Redaction and information loss

Skilled case 04, round 2 recorded 49 `host-path` replacements across stored representations.
This count does not mean 49 distinct sensitive values. The session retained a candidate and
passed artifact-integrity and machine workflow checks. All three local report variants displayed
the redaction limitation and affected locations.

The current security-review policy, informed by OWASP LLM02:2025 guidance, requires redaction
before display and persistence. It may withhold material context even from an authorized operator;
OWASP does not specifically mandate hiding data from that operator. Downstream impact remains
`unassessed`, and original-source retrieval remains `unverified` and authorization-dependent.
No unredacted session-artifact copy is guaranteed.

Neither failed session recorded artifact replacements. Most valid skilled findings also remained
`not-established`; the one exception, case 03 in round 2, was `ready-for-human-sign-off`.
That status is not human sign-off or verified completion. Redaction alone does not establish
incompleteness, and its relationship to remaining verification gaps is unassessed.

The local report-presentation checks passed for all three reports, with zero additional
presentation replacements or withheld lines. The publication uses an allowlisted projection
rather than copying those local reports or their private reveal links. Pattern scanning does
not guarantee that every sensitive fact has been detected.
