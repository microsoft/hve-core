# MVE Plan: Synthetic Invoice Matching

Synthetic preservation marker: EV07-PLAN-PRESERVE-001

## Problem statement and context

A synthetic finance tool matches supplier invoices to purchase orders. This record
is evaluation fixture data only and represents no real system or customer.

## Hypotheses

* H1 (priority 1): We believe a deterministic matcher handles the common synthetic
  case. We will test this by replaying a synthetic invoice sample. We will know we
  are right or wrong when at least 80 percent of that sample matches without
  manual review.

## Vetting results

Synthetic vetting recorded no unresolved red flag for this fixture.

## Experiment design

* Type: data feasibility
* Approach: replay a bounded synthetic sample through a prototype matcher.
* Scope: matching only. Payment posting is out of scope.
* Timeline: two weeks.

## Success and failure criteria

* Success: at least 80 percent of the synthetic sample matches without review.
* Failure: below that threshold, which is a valid learning outcome.

## Required resources and team composition

Synthetic: one engineer and one analyst. No real staffing commitment is represented.

## Next steps for both outcomes

* Validated: extend the synthetic prototype to the remaining sample shapes.
* Invalidated: revisit the matching rules before any further synthetic work.

## Evaluation approach and decision criteria

Compare measured match rate against the recorded threshold.

## Iteration plan for mixed or inconclusive results

Refine the rule set once, then re-run the same synthetic sample.
