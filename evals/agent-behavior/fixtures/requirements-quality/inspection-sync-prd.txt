# Field Inspection Sync PRD

## Product Goals

* `GOAL-001`: Technicians complete 95% of inspections without a paper fallback by 2027-03-31.
* `GOAL-002`: Site managers see failed safety items within one business day.

## Functional Requirements

* `FR-001`: The mobile app saves an in-progress checklist on the device. Traces to `GOAL-001`.
* `FR-002`: The mobile app attaches up to 10 photos to a checklist item. Traces to `GOAL-004`.

## Acceptance Criteria

* `AC-001` (for `FR-001`): Given the app closes unexpectedly, when the technician reopens it, then the in-progress checklist is restored without data loss.
* `AC-002` (for `FR-002`): Given a checklist item, when the technician attaches a tenth photo, then the app accepts it and rejects an eleventh.

## Non-Functional Requirements

* `NFR-001`: The app restores an in-progress checklist within 2 seconds on a mid-range device.

## Design Decisions

* `DD-001`: Photos are stored on the device until the checklist is submitted.
