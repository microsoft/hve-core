# Field Inspection Sync BRD

## Business Goals

* `BG-001`: Reduce inspection rework caused by lost paper checklists from 12% to 4% of completed inspections by 2027-06-30.

## Functional Requirements

* `FR-001`: The service records each completed inspection checklist against its site identifier. Traces to `BG-001`.
* `FR-002`: The service notifies the site manager when an inspection records a failed safety item. Traces to `BG-001`.

## Acceptance Criteria

* `AC-001` (for `FR-001`): Given a technician completes a checklist for site `S-104`, when the checklist is submitted, then the record is retrievable by site identifier within 5 seconds.

## Non-Functional Requirements

* `NFR-001`: The service retains inspection records for 7 years.

## Constraints

* `CON-001`: The regional safety regulator requires inspection records to stay within the EU data boundary.
