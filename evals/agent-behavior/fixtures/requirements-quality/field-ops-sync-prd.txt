# Field Ops Sync PRD

## Product Goals

* `GOAL-001`: Dispatchers see completed inspections without manual uploads.

## Functional Requirements

* `FR-001`: Technicians record inspections offline on the mobile app. Traces to `GOAL-001`.
* `FR-002`: The dispatcher dashboard lists synchronized inspections by site. Traces to `GOAL-001`.
* `FR-003`: The mobile app synchronizes completed inspections to the dispatch service daily. Traces to `GOAL-001`.

## Acceptance Criteria

* `AC-001` (for `FR-001`): Given no network, when a technician completes an inspection, then it is saved on the device.
* `AC-002` (for `FR-002`): Given synchronized inspections, when a dispatcher filters by site, then only that site's inspections are listed.
* `AC-003` (for `FR-003`): Given completed inspections on the device, when the weekly batch runs on Sunday, then the inspections are synchronized once per week.
