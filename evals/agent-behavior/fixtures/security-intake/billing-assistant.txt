# Billing assistant

* Purpose: a customer-facing chatbot that answers billing questions.
* Model: a hosted large language model generates every reply without human review.
* Presentation: replies appear under the name "Billing Team"; customers are not told the replies are machine-generated.
* Escalation: there is no route to a human agent.
* Data: full conversation transcripts, including the last four digits of payment cards, are kept for seven years.
